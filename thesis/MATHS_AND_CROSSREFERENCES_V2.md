# Mathematics, concepts, and cross-reference revision

## Conceptual corrections

- Separated the heuristic quantity `H^(c) = (LQ)S - B`, the higher-order latent
  variable `zeta`, and the estimated factor score `H-hat_i`. They are related,
  but they are not mathematically identical.
- Oriented every fitted domain in the capability-positive direction. Systemic
  burden indicators are reversed before modelling, avoiding a hidden sign error
  in higher-order loadings and composite weights.
- Made the SEM factor score the primary metric. The weighted composite is limited
  to the pre-specified head-to-head sensitivity analysis.
- Added a decision gate: if a one-factor summary is not supported, report the
  three-domain capability profile rather than forcing a scalar score.

## Mathematical corrections

- Replaced all 15 derivation placeholders with explicit mathematics covering the
  conceptual-to-SEM bridge, identification, factor-score error, invariance,
  Delphi consensus, recalibration, Cox partial likelihood, Schoenfeld residuals,
  concordance, calibration, net benefit, Rubin pooling, bootstrap optimism,
  non-circularity, and incremental value.
- Removed the claim that three indicators automatically prove identification.
- Added correlated-factor and bifactor comparisons because three first-order
  factors can make the second-order component just identified.
- Added cause-specific and cumulative-incidence treatment of death as a competing
  event for first hospitalisation.
- Clarified that full-pipeline validation repeats imputation, SEM, scoring, and
  prognosis inside each resample.

## Mathematical novelty extension

- Added Appendix E, the Axiomatic Capability Realisation Model.
- Defined six axioms: dimensional coherence, normalisation, monotonicity,
  bounded realisation, essential enablement, and replication invariance.
- Replaced unrestricted subtraction with a bounded CES/geometric realisation
  family, with the non-compensation parameter `rho` given a substantive meaning.
- Proved admissibility and biomedical-context complementarity.
- Added a characterisation theorem: constant positive domain elasticities,
  normalisation, and regularity uniquely select the weighted geometric CRF.
- Proved the balance penalty using weighted AM--GM, so the loss from an
  enabling-domain bottleneck is a theorem rather than an asserted intuition.
- Derived exact elasticities, marginal gradients, and a three-component
  log-capability-gap decomposition.
- Added an equally distributed equivalent population functional with an
  explicit equity-aversion parameter and its person-level marginal derivative.
- Added a constrained policy-gradient formulation while explicitly separating
  model gradients from causal intervention effects.
- Added reproducible simulations comparing additive, geometric, harmonic, and
  bottleneck-limit specifications. They demonstrate observationally meaningful
  rank changes that an additive score conceals, sensitivity to `rho`, and the
  effect of equity aversion. Figures and machine-readable CSVs are generated by
  `analysis/02_crf_simulation.py`.
- Added `analysis/03_empirical_comparison.R`, a data-dependent scaffold for
  comparing the additive, SEM, and CRF candidates using discrimination,
  calibration, likelihood, decision value, and paired resampling.
- Added a falsification plan comparing the CRF against the additive composite
  and SEM factor score without outcome-tuning its parameters, with explicit
  success, partial-support, and rejection criteria.
- Added `analysis/01_capability_realisation.R` with dependency-free boundary
  checks. The script was syntax-reviewed but could not be executed in the current
  environment because R is unavailable.
