# Chapter 2 -> Empirical/Code Bridge

Chapter 2 now generates explicit empirical obligations rather than merely describing literature.

## Required analysis modules

1. Theory-to-variable registry: indicator, domain, direction, source, rationale, missingness, coding.
2. Competing measurement structures: correlated three-factor, second-order, and bifactor/general-specific where identified.
3. Reflective/formative interrogation: document domain ontology and do not force all indicators into one reflective CFA.
4. Construct validity: convergent, discriminant, factor determinacy, reliability and local dependence.
5. Measurement invariance across major demographic/socioeconomic groups.
6. Comparator validity: intrinsic capacity, frailty, social vulnerability, and conventional health status.
7. Incremental prognostic value: mortality and hospitalisation models entered in pre-specified blocks.
8. Internal validation: bootstrap/cross-validation with all score-estimation and outcome-model uncertainty handled transparently.
9. Failure states: pipeline must permit rejection of a general factor, domain redundancy, non-invariance, or lack of incremental prediction.
10. No outcome leakage: mortality/hospitalisation cannot be used to choose indicators or the latent structure.

## Literature-derived decision rule

A statistically convenient model is not automatically the theoretically correct model. The final score exists only if the measurement evidence, construct validity, comparator analysis and prognostic analysis jointly justify it.
