\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Where} \\
\hline
\endhead
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{The measure and its domains}} \\
$P$ & Biomedical Potential: the domain score for the body's capacity, scaled to $[0,1]$ & Ch.~\ref{ch:theory}, \ref{app:crf} \\
$S$ & Social Modifiers: the domain score for the social conditions that let capacity be used, scaled to $[0,1]$ (in the heuristic $(L\cdot Q)S-B$ of Chapter~\ref{ch:theory}, $S$ is instead a multiplicative social factor) & Ch.~\ref{ch:theory}, \ref{app:crf} \\
$B$ & Systemic Burdens: the domain score for structural constraint, scaled to $[0,1]$, higher meaning more burden & Ch.~\ref{ch:theory}, \ref{app:crf} \\
$R$ & Structural freedom, $R=1-B$ & Ch.~\ref{app:crf} \\
$H$ & Realised capability, the H-Potential score produced by the realisation function from $P$, $S$ and $R$ & Ch.~\ref{app:crf} \\
$\widehat H_i$ & The estimated score for person $i$ & Ch.~\ref{ch:methods} \\
$\alpha$ & Weight given to $S$ against $R$ in the realisation function & Ch.~\ref{app:crf} \\
$\rho$ & Substitution parameter of the CES family: how far one domain can make up for another (in Appendix~\ref{app:dynamical}, $\rho(x,t)$ is a density; see below) & Ch.~\ref{app:crf} \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Measurement model}} \\
$\eta_k$ & Latent score of domain $k$ & Ch.~\ref{ch:methods} \\
$\zeta$ & General (higher-order) factor common to the three domains & Ch.~\ref{ch:methods} \\
$\lambda$ & Factor loading: how strongly an indicator tracks its domain & Ch.~\ref{ch:methods}, \ref{ch:results1} \\
$\omega$ & Composite reliability of a domain & Ch.~\ref{ch:results1} \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Survival and prediction}} \\
$h(t)$ & Hazard: the instantaneous rate of the event at time $t$ among those still at risk & Ch.~\ref{ch:methods}, \ref{ch:results2} \\
$C$ & Concordance (Harrell's or Uno's C-statistic) & Ch.~\ref{ch:results2} \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Heuristic form of Chapter~\ref{ch:theory}}} \\
$L$ & Life expectancy adjusted for disease severity (years) & Ch.~\ref{ch:theory} \\
$Q$ & Quality-of-life multiplier, so that $L\cdot Q$ is quality-adjusted survival & Ch.~\ref{ch:theory} \\
$T$ & Treatment input & App.~\ref{app:dynamical} \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Dynamical extension and haemophilia}} \\
$X(t)$ & Disease marker concentration, the general state variable & App.~\ref{app:dynamical} \\
$S_T$ & Clotting factor concentration (the haemophilia form of $X$) & App.~\ref{app:hemo_dynamics} \\
$Y$ & Inhibitor titre (Bethesda units) & App.~\ref{app:hemo_dynamics} \\
$\kappa$ & Efficacy of immune tolerance induction in suppressing the inhibitor & App.~\ref{app:hemo_dynamics} \\
$Z$ & Homeostatic or restorative factor & App.~\ref{app:dynamical} \\
$dW_t$ & Increment of a Wiener process, the source of random variation & App.~\ref{app:dynamical} \\
$\rho(x,t)$ & Density of patient states across a cohort & App.~\ref{app:dynamical} \\
