# Literature Audit — Core Bridges and Critical Questions

| Literature | What it establishes | What the thesis should do with it |
|---|---|---|
| WHO healthy ageing / intrinsic capacity | Functional ability emerges from intrinsic capacity interacting with environment. | Treat this as the closest conceptual predecessor; explain exactly what H-Potential adds. |
| Sen / health-capability literature | Capability concerns substantive opportunities, not merely achieved functionings. | Use carefully; do not equate health capability with total wellbeing or freedom. |
| QALY / DALY / HALE | Established ways to combine duration, quality, disability and healthy life expectancy. | Critique scope and purpose, not validity. Avoid straw-man arguments. |
| Frailty index | Deficit accumulation can represent latent vulnerability and predict outcomes. | Make frailty a serious comparator and test incremental validity. |
| ATHLOS health metrics | Latent multidimensional health metrics can be developed with CFA/IRT and validated longitudinally. | Explicitly acknowledge this prior art; novelty must lie elsewhere. |
| Intrinsic-capacity studies | General capacity factors can predict later disability and adverse outcomes. | Explain why adding social/structural conditions is theoretically distinct and testable. |
| ICECAP | Capability can be operationalised as broader wellbeing and is not identical to health status. | Establish discriminant validity; prevent H-Potential becoming a wellbeing score. |
| Social vulnerability indices | Social disadvantage can be aggregated and predicts mortality/disability independently of frailty. | Treat social vulnerability as a direct comparator and conceptual neighbour. |
| Social determinants / life-course theory | Social position, cumulative exposure and institutional conditions shape health trajectories. | Build causal/contextual bridges rather than listing socioeconomic variables. |
| Intersectionality | Social categories can interact and produce non-additive forms of disadvantage. | Use this to motivate invariance and subgroup analysis, not to imply every interaction belongs in the latent score. |

## Central literature question

The thesis should repeatedly ask:

> Is H-Potential genuinely a new health-capability construct, or is it a statistical recombination of intrinsic capacity, frailty, social vulnerability and current functioning?

The answer must come from comparative measurement and validation, not from assertion.
