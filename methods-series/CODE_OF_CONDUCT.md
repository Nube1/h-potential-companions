# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project.
- Show empathy towards other people.

Things that do not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scientific conduct

This project backs published manuscripts, so two further standards apply.

- **Report what you ran.** Do not claim a number reproduces unless you have run
  the assertion.
- **Say what you do not know.** A limitation stated plainly is worth more than a
  result overstated.

## Our responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards and are expected to
take fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behaviour. They have the
right to remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do
not meet this code, and to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for
behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and in public spaces when someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour by opening a
confidential issue or contacting a maintainer. Every complaint will be reviewed
and investigated, and will get a response appropriate to the circumstances.
Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
