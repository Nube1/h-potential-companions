## Corrections made in companion manuscripts

- No conversion of latent scores into QALYs without calibration.
- Constant elasticity does not imply constant marginal returns; geometric marginal returns already diminish.
- Positive cross-partials are a model property, not proof of intersectionality.
- A scoring gradient is separated from a causal intervention response.
- Exact log-gap identity is for comparable individual profiles or mean log scores; it is not a generic decomposition of arithmetic group means.
- At P=0 arithmetic/geometric equality holds regardless of context, an exception to the usual S=R equality condition.
- High correlation is not sufficient for unbiased or invariant measurement.
- A three-domain second-order covariance model may be just identified, so higher-order fit alone cannot settle dimensionality.
- Zero-score EDE limits are stated exactly rather than hidden by an epsilon floor.
- A failure to show improvement is not a formal non-inferiority test without a justified margin and paired uncertainty.
